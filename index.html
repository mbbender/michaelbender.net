THE ISSUE, WITHOUT THE JARGON

Your site can look fine.
And still be exposed.

PHP is the software behind many websites and business applications. Newly disclosed security flaws, known as PHP2Shell, can let attackers take control of an affected server without signing in.

The problem is in the software underneath your site. Updating a page, changing a password, or having good website code does not fix it. The server needs to be checked and properly updated.

A FEW THINGS YOU MAY BE WONDERING

Good questions.
Straight answers.

Still unsure? Let's talk it through ↗

My website looks fine. Do I still need to act?

Yes, you should confirm whether it is protected. This issue can affect the server without changing how your website looks. An assessment checks the PHP version and any fixes your hosting provider has applied.

Does updating WordPress fix this?

A WordPress update alone does not update the PHP software on your server. Your hosting provider may have applied the fix already. I can help confirm that and coordinate any work still needed.

Our original developer is gone. Can you help?

Yes. Start with what you have: your website address, hosting provider, or the name of the application. We'll work out how it is hosted and what access is needed. Don't send passwords through the form.

Will you have to take my website offline?

That depends on the exposure and how safely it can be updated. I'll explain the options and any expected interruption before we agree on the work. If the risk calls for a temporary pause, we'll discuss it directly.

How quickly can this be fixed?

It depends on your hosting, PHP version, application, and whether there are signs of a compromise. A straightforward update may take a few hours; older or affected systems can need more work. Call me to discuss urgency and current availability.

What if my host says we're already protected?

Ask them to confirm that the fix is applied to the PHP version serving your website, including any vendor-provided patch. If you have written confirmation and everything is covered, you may not need additional work. I'm happy to help you understand their answer.
