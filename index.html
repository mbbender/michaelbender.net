Quick answers

Questions owners ask

What is PHP2Shell?

Two critical flaws in PHP, the software that runs a huge share of the web, including most WordPress sites. They were made public and patched on October 21, 2026 (CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619). An attacker can take over the server behind your website with a single web request and no password.

My code is solid. Am I still at risk?

Yes. The flaw is in PHP itself, not in your website's code. A perfectly written site on an unpatched server is still exposed.

How do I know if my site uses PHP?

If it runs on WordPress, Drupal, Magento, Joomla, Laravel or most shared hosting, it almost certainly does. Send me the address and I will tell you.
